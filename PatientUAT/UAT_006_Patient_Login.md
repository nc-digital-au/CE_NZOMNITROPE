Use Case:
A registered patient logs in to access the OmniPal Patient Support Program portal.

Test:
Patient login - successful login

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient has a registered account with valid credentials
- The patient is not currently logged in

Instructions:
1. Navigate to the OmniPal landing page.
2. Click the "LOGIN" button.
3. Enter the registered email address and password in the login form.
4. Submit the login form.

Pass Criteria:
- The patient is successfully authenticated.
- The patient is redirected to the Home dashboard.
- The Home dashboard displays the welcome message and available actions (Order SurePal Device, Schedule Training, Watch Video, Resources).
