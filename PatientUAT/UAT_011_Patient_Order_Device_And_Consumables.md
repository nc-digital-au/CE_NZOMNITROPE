Use Case:
A patient orders a SurePal device and consumables (needle kit and/or pen replacement) for the first time or after 90 days from their last order.

Test:
Patient order SurePal device and consumables - full options

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal
- The patient has not placed an order in the last 90 days, OR this is their first order

Instructions:
1. From the Home dashboard, click "Order SurePal® Device and Consumables".
2. On the Patient Details step, verify pre-populated patient information (first name, last name, NHI number, email, mobile).
3. Select Delivery Address Type (Place of business or Private address).
4. If Place of business, enter or verify the business name.
5. Enter or verify the delivery address (unit number, street address, city, postcode).
6. Click "Next" to proceed to Order Form.
7. Select ONE option from the Needle Kit Options (4mm, 5mm, or 8mm Needle Kit).
8. Alternatively, select ONE option from Pen Replacement (5mg, 10mg, or 15mg Pen).
9. Click "Submit" to place the order.

Pass Criteria:
- A success message appears: "Thank you, your order has been placed and will be delivered within 10 working days."
- The message includes information about pharmacy collection if applicable.
- Contact number for queries (0800 666 487) is displayed.
- The order is recorded in the system.
