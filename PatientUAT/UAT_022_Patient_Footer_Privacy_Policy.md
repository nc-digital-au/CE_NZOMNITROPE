Use Case:
A patient accesses the Privacy Policy from the footer of the application.

Test:
Patient footer - Privacy Policy link

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal

Instructions:
1. Navigate to any page within the authenticated portal (e.g., Home dashboard).
2. Scroll to the footer of the page.
3. Click on the "Privacy Policy" link.

Pass Criteria:
- A leaving site dialog or confirmation may appear (if implemented).
- The Privacy Policy page opens in a new tab/window.
- The Sandoz Privacy Policy page (https://www.sandoz.com.au/Website-privacy-policy/) is displayed.
