Use Case:
A new patient registers for the OmniPal Patient Support Program after being prescribed Omnitrope by their healthcare professional.

Test:
Patient registration - successful registration

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- Patient registration feature is available in the application
- The patient has collected their prescription and has access to the product barcode (last 4 digits)
- The email address to be used has not been previously registered

Instructions:
1. Navigate to the OmniPal landing page.
2. Click the "REGISTER" button.
3. On the Welcome step, enter the last 4 digits of the product barcode.
4. Check the confirmation checkbox "I confirm that I have been prescribed Omnitrope (somatropin)."
5. Click "Next" to proceed to Patient Details.
6. Enter patient details including first name, last name, NHI number, email address, mobile phone, and password.
7. Click "Next" to proceed to Guardian Details.
8. Optionally enter guardian details (first name, last name, email, mobile phone) or leave blank.
9. Click "Next" to proceed to Delivery step.
10. Enter the name of person collecting the delivery and contact number.
11. Enter delivery address details (unit number if applicable, street address, city, postcode).
12. Click "Next" to proceed to Terms step.
13. Read and accept the privacy consent, terms and conditions, and adverse event contact consent.
14. Click "Submit" to complete registration.

Pass Criteria:
- A success message appears: "Thank you for registering, your account is now active."
- The patient receives a confirmation email (check spam/junk folder if not in inbox).
- The patient can log in with the registered email and password.
- The patient record is created in the system.
