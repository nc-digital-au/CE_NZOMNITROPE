Use Case:
A patient attempts to log in with incorrect credentials.

Test:
Patient login - invalid credentials error

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient has a registered account
- The patient is not currently logged in

Instructions:
1. Navigate to the OmniPal landing page.
2. Click the "LOGIN" button.
3. Enter an incorrect email address or password.
4. Submit the login form.

Pass Criteria:
- An error message is displayed indicating invalid credentials.
- The patient is NOT logged in.
- The patient remains on the login page.
