Use Case:
A patient accesses the Contact Us information from the footer of the application.

Test:
Patient footer - Contact Us dialog

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal

Instructions:
1. Navigate to any page within the authenticated portal (e.g., Home dashboard).
2. Scroll to the footer of the page.
3. Click on the "Contact Us" link.

Pass Criteria:
- A Contact Us dialog appears.
- The dialog displays the Program Administrator contact number (0800 666 487).
- The dialog displays medical enquiry/adverse event reporting information with email (mi.new_zealand@sandoz.com) and phone (0800 726 369).
- The dialog displays product complaint information with email (sdz.technicalcomplaint@sandoz.com).
