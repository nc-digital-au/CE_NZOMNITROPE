Use Case:
A patient logs out of the OmniPal Patient Support Program portal.

Test:
Patient logout

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal

Instructions:
1. Navigate to any page within the authenticated portal.
2. Locate the logout option (typically in the header or user menu).
3. Click the logout option.

Pass Criteria:
- The patient is logged out of the system.
- The patient is redirected to the landing page.
- Attempting to access authenticated pages redirects to the login/landing page.
- The patient's session is terminated.
