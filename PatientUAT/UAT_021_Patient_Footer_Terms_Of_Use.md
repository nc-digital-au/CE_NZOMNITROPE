Use Case:
A patient accesses the Terms of Use from the footer of the application.

Test:
Patient footer - Terms of Use link

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal

Instructions:
1. Navigate to any page within the authenticated portal (e.g., Home dashboard).
2. Scroll to the footer of the page.
3. Click on the "Terms of Use" link.

Pass Criteria:
- A leaving site dialog or confirmation may appear (if implemented).
- The Terms of Use page opens in a new tab/window.
- The Sandoz Terms of Use page (https://www.sandoz.com.au/terms-use-and-conditions/) is displayed.
