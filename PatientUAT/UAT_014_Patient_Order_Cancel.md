Use Case:
A patient cancels the order process before submission.

Test:
Patient order - cancel order

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal
- The patient has started the order process

Instructions:
1. From the Home dashboard, click "Order SurePal® Device and Consumables".
2. Begin filling out the order form (any step).
3. Click the "Cancel" button.

Pass Criteria:
- The patient is redirected back to the Home dashboard.
- No order is recorded in the system.
- Any entered data is discarded.
