Use Case:
A patient watches the "How to Inject" instructional video from the Home dashboard.

Test:
Patient view how to inject video

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal

Instructions:
1. From the Home dashboard, locate the "Watch: How to Inject Video" section.
2. Click the play button.
3. View the video on the resources page.

Pass Criteria:
- The patient is navigated to the How to Inject resources page.
- The video player (Kaltura embedded video) is displayed.
- The video is playable and shows injection instructions.
- The page title "How to Inject" is displayed.
