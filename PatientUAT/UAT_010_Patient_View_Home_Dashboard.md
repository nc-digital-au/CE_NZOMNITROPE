Use Case:
A logged-in patient views the Home dashboard to see available program services.

Test:
Patient view home dashboard

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal

Instructions:
1. Navigate to or confirm you are on the Home page after login.
2. Review the displayed content and available options.

Pass Criteria:
- The welcome message "Welcome to the OmniPal Patient Support Program" is displayed.
- The "Order SurePal® Device and Consumables" button is displayed and clickable.
- The "Schedule an Injection Training Session" button is displayed and clickable.
- The "Watch: How to Inject Video" section with a play button is displayed.
- The Resources section with links to SurePal guides (5mg, 10mg, 15mg) and Consumer Medicine Information is displayed.
