Use Case:
A patient cancels the training scheduling process before submission.

Test:
Patient schedule training - cancel request

Preconditions/Instructions:

Preconditions:
- The patient is logged in to the OmniPal portal
- The patient has started the training scheduling process

Instructions:
1. From the Home dashboard, click "Schedule an Injection Training Session".
2. Begin filling out the training request form.
3. Click the "Cancel" button.

Pass Criteria:
- The patient is redirected back to the Home dashboard.
- No training request is recorded in the system.
- Any entered data is discarded.
