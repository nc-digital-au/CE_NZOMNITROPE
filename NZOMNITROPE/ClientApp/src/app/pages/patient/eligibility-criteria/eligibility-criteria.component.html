Patient Eligibility Criteria

To be considered for the Boehringer Ingelheim Medicines Request Program, patients must fulfill the following eligibility criteria:

  • Patient is on the transplant list.
  • Patient is not eligible for an ILD clinical trial.
  • MDT Consensus of advanced IPF/PF-ILD
  • MDT consensus to initiate OFEV to stabilise patient for transplant.

For the full list of contraindications, special warnings, precautions, and potential side effects, please refer to the OFEV® Product Information at before prescribing.
